Martin Cousineau is an associate professor in the Department of Logistics and Operations Management at HEC Montréal and an IVADO professor. He is a regular member of CIRRELT, co-head of the Sustainable Health research theme at Obvia and an officer of the Health Care Operational Research SIG of CORS. His expertise lies in methods at the intersection of operations research and machine learning, with a focus on logistics, transportation and healthcare applications.

Research

  • Gamification of online stochastic optimization problems. Turning dynamic logistics problems (vehicle routing with stochastic requests, electric-vehicle home charging) into Atari-like games solved with machine learning. Play the games · See: IJOC 2026, Press Start to Charge (working paper)
  • Integration of learning and optimization. Integrating machine learning into optimization methods to solve more realistic problems: deep reinforcement learning for electric ride-hailing fleets, learning planners' implicit knowledge in routing, surveillance tours for gas networks, implicit time windows in the traveling salesman problem. See: Transportation Science 2022, NeuroPlanner (working paper), Transportation Research Procedia 2025
  • Healthcare operations and analytics. Causal inference and decision support for mental-health services, hospital supply chains and the adoption of innovative medical devices. See: EJOR 2023, AJMC 2024, IJPR 2025
  • Empirical research in supply chains. How geopolitical tensions reshape sourcing (friendshoring, decoupling), using large-scale trade data. See: IJOPM 2025, IJOPM 2026
