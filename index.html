Martin Cousineau is an associate professor in the Department of Logistics and Operations Management at HEC Montréal and an IVADO professor. He is a regular member of CIRRELT and co-head of the Sustainable Health research theme at Obvia. His expertise lies in methods at the intersection of operations research and machine learning, with a focus on logistics, transportation and healthcare applications.

Research

  • Gamification of online stochastic optimization problems. Turning dynamic logistics problems (vehicle routing with stochastic requests, electric-vehicle home charging) into Atari-like games solved with machine learning. See: INFORMS Journal on Computing 2026, Press Start to Charge (working paper)
  • Integration of learning and optimization. Integrating machine learning into optimization methods to solve more realistic problems: deep reinforcement learning for electric ride-hailing fleets, learning planners' implicit knowledge in routing, surveillance tours for gas networks, implicit time windows in the traveling salesman problem. See: Transportation Science 2022, NeuroPlanner (working paper), Transportation Research Procedia 2025
  • Healthcare operations and analytics. Causal inference and decision support for mental-health services, hospital supply chains and the adoption of innovative medical devices. See: European Journal of Operational Research 2023, The American Journal of Managed Care 2024, International Journal of Production Research 2025
  • Empirical research in supply chains. How geopolitical tensions reshape sourcing (friendshoring, decoupling), using large-scale trade data. See: International Journal of Operations & Production Management 2025, International Journal of Operations & Production Management 2026
