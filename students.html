Students

Prospective students

I have no funded positions open at the moment. I remain happy to hear from students with their own funding or scholarship (e.g., FRQNT, NSERC, IVADO, Mitacs) who have a quantitative background and are curious about combining optimization and machine learning, from HEC M.Sc. students looking for a supervised project, and from visiting students seeking a research internship. When writing, include a short description of your interests, your CV and transcripts. Write to martin.cousineau@hec.ca; HEC M.Sc. students may also find my note on writing a supervised project report useful.
